# Start Here: HackIndia 2026–27

Welcome to the HackIndia 2026–27 circuit. Event rules, dates, team sizes, prizes, and tracks can vary by hackathon, so always use the **specific event page** as the source of truth.

## 1. Choose Your Hackathon

[Browse HackIndia Hackathons](https://hackindia.org/hackathons)

Check the event page for:
- Date and venue
- Online or in-person format
- Registration deadline
- Team size
- Entry fee, if any
- Prize pool and sponsor rewards
- Official tracks and problem statements

## 2. Register on HackIndia

Create or use your HackIndia account and register for the selected event.

> If the event page asks every team member to register individually, each member must complete their own registration.

## 3. Form Your Team

Build a team that covers the skills you need: development, AI/ML, Web3, cybersecurity, hardware/IoT, UI/UX, product, or presentation.

Follow the **team-size limit shown on your event page**.

## 4. Select a Track

Depending on the event, you may see:
- AI & AI Agents
- Generative AI / ML
- Web3 & Blockchain
- Cybersecurity
- Hardware, IoT & Embedded Systems
- Open Innovation

## 5. Understand the Problem

Use official problem statements when provided. If an event supports Open Innovation, define a clear real-world problem and explain who has it, why it matters, your solution, and what makes it better.

## 6. Build a Working Prototype

Focus on a demonstrable product rather than only slides or an idea.

Ideally include:
- Working prototype or live application
- Clear user flow
- Technical architecture
- README and setup instructions
- Demo data or test cases
- Short demo video when required

## 7. Submit Before the Deadline

Verify the event-specific submission requirements and make sure all required links, files, team details, and declarations are complete.

## 8. Prepare Your Pitch

Be ready to explain:

**Problem → Users → Solution → Technology → Demo → Innovation → Impact → Scalability → Future**

### The HackIndia mindset

**Build. Innovate. Compete. Launch.** 🚀
