# Track Guidelines — 2026–27

HackIndia runs multiple hackathons during the 2026–27 season. **Not every event has the same tracks**, so check your event page before selecting a track.

## 🤖 AI & AI Agents

Build intelligent systems using AI, machine learning, generative AI, LLMs, RAG, computer vision, speech, or autonomous agents.

### Example directions
- AI assistants and copilots
- Autonomous or multi-agent systems
- AI for education and healthcare
- Productivity and workflow automation
- Generative AI applications
- Developer tools and AI infrastructure

## ⛓️ Web3 & Blockchain

Build decentralized applications, protocols, or digital experiences using blockchain and Web3 technologies.

### Example directions
- Decentralized identity
- Smart contracts and dApps
- Tokenized assets
- Digital ownership
- DeFi and financial infrastructure
- Blockchain-based verification

## 🛡️ Cybersecurity

Build technology that improves security, privacy, trust, and resilience.

### Example directions
- Threat detection
- Fraud detection
- Identity and access management
- Privacy-preserving systems
- Vulnerability detection
- Network security
- Digital forensics
- Secure applications

## 🔧 Hardware, IoT & Embedded Systems

Where supported by the event, build connected or embedded solutions that interact with the physical world.

### Example directions
- Smart sensors
- Edge AI
- Industrial monitoring
- Smart energy systems
- Robotics
- Safety and environmental monitoring

## 💡 Open Innovation

Some HackIndia events allow teams to define their own problem.

You may work on:
- Climate and sustainability
- Accessibility
- Education
- Healthcare
- FinTech
- Productivity
- Smart cities
- Developer tools

## Choosing the Right Track

If your project combines multiple technologies, choose the track that represents its **primary value proposition**.

> Always follow the track definitions and eligibility rules published on your specific HackIndia event page.
