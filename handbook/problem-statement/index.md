# 🧩 Problem Statements — 2026–27

Problem statements are **event-specific** in the HackIndia 2026–27 circuit.

## Where to find the official challenges

Open your HackIndia event page and check its **Problem statements** section:

[Browse HackIndia Hackathons](https://hackindia.org/hackathons)

Some events publish fixed challenges, while others allow Open Innovation submissions.

## If Your Event Has Given Problems

Choose a challenge that:
- Matches your team's skills
- Has a clear user or beneficiary
- Can be prototyped within the available hackathon time
- Allows you to demonstrate measurable impact
- Gives you room to show technical depth

## If Your Event Has Open Innovation

Define your own challenge using this structure:

**Problem:** What is broken or inefficient?

**Users:** Who experiences the problem?

**Evidence:** Why is this a real problem?

**Solution:** What will you build?

**Technology:** Why are your chosen technologies appropriate?

**Impact:** What measurable improvement can your solution create?

**Prototype:** What will you demonstrate during judging?

> Do not copy problem statements from an older HackIndia edition. Use the challenge published on your current 2026–27 event page.
